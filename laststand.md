# last stand strategy

Laststand is a high-difficulty gameplay mode in Bloxd, similar to a game where you have to survive 99 waves. Here, I will write down some strategies that I believe are effective.  

## maps  
There are four maps in total: 
maps:
- lastStandBox: The earliest map added, featuring a closed cross-shaped passage. Monster waves spawn at the four vertices of the cross, while the central four corners are occupied by merchants and enchantment tables.
- Garden: An open map featuring a lush green garden.  
- Castle: A closed castle, with strange tide generation points on all four sides, and high platforms and pillars at the four corners in the center. Each corner of the room houses a merchant and a full --set of enchantment tables.

## Mob  

1.**Zombies**:  
In Laststand, the weakest and most common small monster has a slow movement speed and medium attack power, but it's best to shoot it.  
In the later stages, its presence is extremely low and it's used just to make up the numbers.  

2.**Skeletons**:
First appearing in the second round, this is the most common small remote-shooting monster.   
It moves slowly and may even "kill itself". Its speed is very slow, making it almost a filler monster.  

3.**cave golem**:
This monster is relatively large, easy to hit, and has high attack power.   
However, it has many disadvantages: it is not good at climbing stairs, its speed is extremely slow, and it is prone to killing skeletons.  
It is recommended to use bows and arrows.  

4.**wolf**:
Although the health is low and the damage is low, it is very small and difficult to be hit by arrows.   
It is also easy to be hit with a sword and has a fast attack speed.
Try to use arrows and fireballs as much as possible. It is a annoying monster.

5.**stag**:
A powerful monster with strong attack power, able to fly far away, fast speed, and difficult to deal with.
Mainly appearing from the 8th round onwards, it is difficult to be knockback.
Close combat is not feasible and can only be done with bow.

6.**bear**:
Similar to stags in terms of damage and speed, as well as health and fly ability, but the downside is that it is difficult to climb stairs.  

7.**fire Golem**:
lots of health, capable of firing fireballs, high damage, high degree of iknockback, difficult to repel, and relatively fast to climb stairs. 
It is best to take a chili or fire-resistant potion to deal with it. This monster has scared many players.

8.**draugr knight**:
Although the player's health is not high, they have strong attack power, fast speed, and can jump high.   
Sometimes they can hit the invincible point, but due to their fast speed, they are almost impossible to shoot with a bow and arrow under normal circumstances.  
However, sometimes it rushes towards the wall like crazy, and even if you hit it, it won't turn back, making it easy to attack.  

## Upgrades  

Each game will offer random boosts for players to purchase, with a fixed price of one moonstone.
After purchasing a boost, an icon item for the boost will appear in the backpack, and multiple boosts can be stacked to take effect. 
Players can refresh their purchase of boosts by spending 15 levels of experience.
